# Dated finding and scope

The source reports that its search on **2026-11-02** found no published
retention, opt-out or churn data for ChatGPT Pulse, ChatGPT Scheduled tasks,
Gemini scheduled actions, Claude routines/Cowork tasks, OpenClaw or Hermes.
This is a finding about that investigation's coverage, **not proof that no
such data exists**, privately or publicly, for these or all personal-agent
products.

The captured note preserves the search date and product list, but not a
reproducible query protocol or exhaustive source inventory. The harvest did
not inspect the full research report or independently repeat the search.
Treat retention as unresolved in this evidence set, not as measured zero
adoption or measured abandonment.

# What the available proxies cannot establish

The source encountered product-lifecycle reports, GitHub popularity counts,
secondary usage reporting and self-selected discussion threads. Those are
different evidence types, not interchangeable retention measurements:

- A retirement or replacement does not establish why people stopped using
  a product, or how many did so.
- GitHub stars are not retained usage. An aggregate usage or traffic change
  is not, without the required cohort evidence, a retention estimate.
- Self-selected complaints can suggest hypotheses about cost, reliability
  or trust; they do not establish their prevalence or causal contribution
  to abandonment.

A future comparison should label these proxies explicitly rather than say
that people keep or abandon a personal-agent stack on their authority. Do
not infer that one fronting assistant retains users better than per-agent
threads: this investigation establishes no controlled comparison, consistent
with the separate [supervision-outcomes evidence limit](/nodes/market-research-expert/lessons/supervision-patterns-are-not-outcome-evidence.md).

# Refresh rule

**Owner:** market-research-expert. Recheck when a vendor or survey publishes
retention, opt-out or churn results for scheduled or proactive personal
agents. Before changing the conclusion, identify the population, observation
period and metric; distinguish cohort retention from aggregate activity.
Update this record and the node log when evidence resolves or changes the
gap. No product chronology, adoption rate or reason for retirement is
asserted by this concept.
