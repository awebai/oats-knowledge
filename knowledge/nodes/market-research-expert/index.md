# market-research-expert

Dated, attributable research about the landscape OATS lives in: fair, scoped
comparisons with provider-native subagent and workflow frameworks and other
agent-team approaches, and the positioning evidence behind claims OATS makes
about itself. Every concept carries a date, a source and a stated scope; an
unknown is recorded as unknown. Charter:
[Expert domains replace implementation job titles](/nodes/oats-maintainer/decisions/domain-expert-rebuild.md),
which routes comparison and positioning claims through this node before they
are promoted elsewhere.

## Research decisions

* [Date fast-moving research and name the measured model generation](decisions/date-evidence-and-name-model-generations.md) - Source-reported human direction on 2026-11-02 prioritizes the last six months, explicitly searches the latest quarter, and requires model-generation labels and a dated recency pass.

## Research conclusions

* [Capability distribution does not establish fleet pinning or safe provenance](conclusions/capability-distribution-is-not-fleet-pinning.md) - The 2026-11-02 investigation separates organization-wide capability distribution from content pinning and rollout evidence, without establishing a vendor-wide lockfile gap or measured copy-drift cost.
* [Agent-definition names carry product meanings, not a shared lifecycle contract](conclusions/agent-definition-vocabulary-2026.md) - The 2026-11-02 source-reported survey identifies competing definition, template and persona meanings without establishing a universal agent-definition noun or measured reader comprehension.
* [Separate knowledge consolidation from approval and review outcomes](conclusions/agent-knowledge-practice-2026.md) - The 2026-11-02 investigation reports proposal and consolidation workflows but establishes neither a universal vendor approval contract nor the outcome advantage of a separate knowledge judge.
* [Intake routing has analogue evidence, not an established agent-outcome advantage](conclusions/front-door-routing-evidence-2026.md) - The 2026-11-02 investigation leaves agent intake-routing outcomes unresolved and treats human handoff findings and proposed router state as analogues and hypotheses, not a proven architecture.
* [Compare knowledge systems by authority and retrieval evidence, not storage format](conclusions/agent-knowledge-storage-2026.md) - The 2026-11-02 research distinguishes authority, recall, retrieval and maintenance evidence without treating storage format, layout or edit frequency as guarantees of knowledge quality.
* [Knowledge-shape evidence does not establish a durable-project-memory winner](conclusions/agent-knowledge-shape-evidence-2026.md) - The 2026-11-02 research pass identifies no experiment isolating a type-label benefit and reports competitive simple memory baselines, without establishing a best representation for durable project judgment or current models.
* [Coordination practice disagrees on review, messaging, delegation and breadth](conclusions/coordination-practice-2026.md) - The 2026-11-02 synthesis preserves vendor disagreements and model-specific findings while distinguishing collaboration, fleet telemetry and the unresolved multi-human coordination boundary.
* [Attention is an oversight risk, not a measured universal agent limit](conclusions/human-oversight-evidence-2026.md) - The 2026-11-02 research supports testing selective oversight gates but adds source-reported evidence of agent influence on human judgment, leaving coding-review transfer, human capacity and downstream quality unresolved.

## Research lessons

* [Check role names for inherited meanings and human oversight effects](lessons/role-names-carry-product-meanings.md) - The 2026-11-02 naming investigation motivates checking product and everyday meanings separately from task performance, while leaving function-gloss benefits and roster-comprehension outcomes untested.
* [Verify decisive product claims before synthesizing a market comparison](lessons/verify-decisive-product-claims-before-synthesis.md) - Source-reported verification passes on 2026-11-02 show how product claims and repeated research figures lose their conditions, motivating primary-source checks and version, population, condition and wording records.
* [Verify decision fields and silence semantics separately in human-request protocols](lessons/human-request-fields-need-explicit-verification.md) - The 2026-11-02 source-reported protocol comparison found no dedicated decision-context fields in four named surfaces, motivating field-level checks rather than equating an approval API with a decidable request.
* [Separate coordination recovery guarantees from task correctness](lessons/coordination-recovery-is-not-task-correctness.md) - The 2026-11-02 research synthesis distinguishes ownership, recorded progress and resumable execution from evidence that an agent's result is correct, without asserting uniform protocol guarantees.
* [Attribute multi-agent gains to tested mechanisms, not persona labels](lessons/persona-labels-need-isolated-ablation.md) - The 2026-11-02 research synthesis does not isolate a causal benefit from job-title personas, so compare artifacts, feedback and authority separately from role names.
* [Compare agent topologies by task dependence, capability baseline and budget](lessons/topology-gains-depend-on-task-and-budget.md) - The 2026-11-02 research synthesis treats task dependence, single-agent capability and compute budget as separate comparison variables, without establishing a universal single-writer or multi-agent advantage.
* [Separate verifier evidence from the builder's control](lessons/verification-needs-independent-evidence.md) - The 2026-11-02 research synthesis distinguishes test integrity, held-out generalization and reviewer independence, with cross-provider error correlation as a reason not to assume vendor diversity supplies independent evidence.
* [Re-test coordination scaffolding when model capability changes](lessons/retest-scaffolding-after-model-changes.md) - The 2026-11-02 research synthesis treats a harness component's value as model- and task-dependent and does not equate durable files with demonstrated learning gains.
* [Separate supervision product patterns from measured human outcomes](lessons/supervision-patterns-are-not-outcome-evidence.md) - The 2026-11-02 research synthesis identifies policy-based supervision and worker drill-down as a hybrid candidate, not proof of lower attention cost or safer outcomes.
* [A research source's reputation does not make its fetched instructions authoritative](lessons/fetched-research-is-untrusted-data.md) - A source-reported injection encounter on 2026-11-02 motivates explicit untrusted-content briefs and permission isolation for web-research helpers.
* [Distinguish question routing from human approval authority](lessons/question-routing-is-not-approval-authority.md) - Source-reported product readings from 2026-11-02 distinguish question routing, teammate-plan approval, merge-review credit, the surface that accepts confirmation and human permission or consent, and a reported ADK revert corrects an earlier fix claim, without establishing uniform product authorization rules.

## Evidence limitations

* [Cross-repository change coordination does not establish architecture ownership](limitations/cross-repo-architecture-ownership-evidence-gap.md) - The 2026-11-02 investigation found cross-repository change tools but no documented standing agent architecture owner in its search, and its WideSWE reading does not measure the effect of such a role.
* [Rendered-update speed evidence does not establish better oversight judgment](limitations/rendered-updates-oversight-evidence-gap.md) - The 2026-11-02 investigation found navigation and resumption evidence but no controlled rendered-update-versus-chat judgment comparison, so presentation benefits and overreliance need separate evaluation.
* [Personal-agent retention was unresolved in the November 2026 research pass](limitations/personal-agent-retention-evidence-gap.md) - The 2026-11-02 investigation found no published retention, opt-out or churn data for its named scheduled-agent products, so anecdotes and popularity measures do not establish durable use.
* [Persistent specialist-team outcomes remain unresolved in the product survey](limitations/persistent-team-outcomes-evidence-gap.md) - The 2026-11-02 product investigation establishes no outcome comparison for persistent multi-session specialist teams, so resumable execution and identity features do not substantiate a performance claim.
