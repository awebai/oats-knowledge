# market-research-expert

Dated, attributable research about the landscape OATS lives in: fair, scoped
comparisons with provider-native subagent and workflow frameworks and other
agent-team approaches, and the positioning evidence behind claims OATS makes
about itself. Every concept carries a date, a source and a stated scope; an
unknown is recorded as unknown. Charter:
[Expert domains replace implementation job titles](/nodes/oats-maintainer/decisions/domain-expert-rebuild.md),
which routes comparison and positioning claims through this node before they
are promoted elsewhere.

## Research conclusions

* [Coordination practice disagrees on review, messaging, delegation and breadth](conclusions/coordination-practice-2026.md) - A 2026-11-02 research synthesis records four live disagreements in vendor coordination practice and agent-count and equal-token findings, linking the topology, verification and persona lessons for the claims they own.
* [Attention is an oversight risk, not a measured universal agent limit](conclusions/human-oversight-evidence-2026.md) - The 2026-11-02 research pass motivates a selective-gate hypothesis and leaves sustainable agents per human and downstream change quality unresolved; supervision-interface evidence is owned by the supervision lesson.

## Research lessons

* [Verify decisive product claims before synthesizing a market comparison](lessons/verify-decisive-product-claims-before-synthesis.md) - A source-reported verification pass on 2026-11-02 narrowed product-status and authority claims, motivating primary-source checks and explicit evidence labels before synthesis.
* [Separate coordination recovery guarantees from task correctness](lessons/coordination-recovery-is-not-task-correctness.md) - The 2026-11-02 research synthesis distinguishes ownership, recorded progress and resumable execution from evidence that an agent's result is correct, without asserting uniform protocol guarantees.
* [Attribute multi-agent gains to tested mechanisms, not persona labels](lessons/persona-labels-need-isolated-ablation.md) - The 2026-11-02 research synthesis does not isolate a causal benefit from job-title personas, so compare artifacts, feedback and authority separately from role names.
* [Compare agent topologies by task dependence and compute budget](lessons/topology-gains-depend-on-task-and-budget.md) - The 2026-11-02 research synthesis favors testing coordinated parallel reading separately from coupled writes and does not establish a universal single-writer or multi-agent advantage.
* [Separate verifier evidence from the builder's control](lessons/verification-needs-independent-evidence.md) - The 2026-11-02 research synthesis distinguishes test integrity, held-out generalization and reviewer independence rather than treating self-review or a second agent as sufficient verification.
* [Re-test coordination scaffolding when model capability changes](lessons/retest-scaffolding-after-model-changes.md) - The 2026-11-02 research synthesis treats a harness component's value as model- and task-dependent and does not equate durable files with demonstrated learning gains.
* [Separate supervision product patterns from measured human outcomes](lessons/supervision-patterns-are-not-outcome-evidence.md) - The 2026-11-02 research synthesis identifies policy-based supervision and worker drill-down as a hybrid candidate, not proof of lower attention cost or safer outcomes.
* [A research source's reputation does not make its fetched instructions authoritative](lessons/fetched-research-is-untrusted-data.md) - A source-reported injection encounter on 2026-11-02 motivates explicit untrusted-content briefs and permission isolation for web-research helpers.
* [Distinguish question routing from human approval authority](lessons/question-routing-is-not-approval-authority.md) - Two source-reported product examples from 2026-11-02 distinguish routing a request from an agent supplying human confirmation, without establishing a universal ban on relayed authorization.

## Evidence limitations

* [Personal-agent retention was unresolved in the November 2026 research pass](limitations/personal-agent-retention-evidence-gap.md) - The 2026-11-02 investigation found no published retention, opt-out or churn data for its named scheduled-agent products, so anecdotes and popularity measures do not establish durable use.
* [Persistent specialist-team outcomes remain unresolved in the product survey](limitations/persistent-team-outcomes-evidence-gap.md) - The 2026-11-02 product investigation establishes no outcome comparison for persistent multi-session specialist teams, so resumable execution and identity features do not substantiate a performance claim.
