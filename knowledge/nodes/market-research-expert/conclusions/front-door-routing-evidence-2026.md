# Dated conclusion

Distinguish **intake routing**—identify an owner, introduce the parties and
step out—from **continued brokering**, which mediates subsequent traffic.
The source's **2026-11-02** investigation identifies this distinction in
vendor guidance [1], but establishes no outcome comparison showing which
works better for agent teams.

The capture does not retain a reproducible product-search inventory or
controlled agent study. Its report that no product combines always-on
intake, a live owner registry and an introduction record is therefore not
promoted as a market-wide absence or OATS uniqueness claim. A coordinator
conversation or handoff feature alone would not answer the outcome question
anyway. The [supervision lesson](/nodes/market-research-expert/lessons/supervision-patterns-are-not-outcome-evidence.md)
owns the broader distinction between interface patterns and human outcomes;
this concept concerns routing correctness, continuity and duplicate effort.

# What the analogues support—and do not support

The source reports older human-system findings on bug reassignment and
learning from routing history, mixed warm-handoff results, and reduced
errors with standardized I-PASS handoff content [2–4]. These are **pre-LLM
human-system analogues**, not measurements of agent routing. Their populations,
interventions and success measures differ, and the short capture does not
retain enough methods to transfer effect sizes or base rates to agents.

They motivate testing how the correct owner is found and what context
survives a transfer. They do not prove that reassignment is always failure,
that a warm introduction is always better, or that a fixed-field handoff
alone prevents loss. The source also flags product channel transitions as a
continuity risk, but its brief product descriptions are not a verified
inventory of dropped-message behavior.

# Candidate state and evaluation, not selected design

The source infers that a router should have an owner registry, an open-effort
ledger, routing history, a duplicate check and a fixed-field introduction.
Treat that as a **design hypothesis**, not an experimentally established
minimum or evidence that these records must live outside every agent's
context. No current-model agent-routing outcome is established by this
capture.

For a comparison or trial, test whether the destination owner is reachable,
whether an existing effort is found before new work starts, and whether the
human knows where subsequent messages go. Measure routing correctness,
continuity after handoff and duplicate work separately from response speed.
Compare the proposed retained state against a simpler baseline rather than
crediting the architecture with benefits inferred from human analogues.
Routing a question also does not grant
[approval authority](/nodes/market-research-expert/lessons/question-routing-is-not-approval-authority.md).

This supports the open [coordination investigation](/nodes/oats-maintainer/stewardship/coordination-redesign-precedes-adaptive-onboarding.md),
without selecting a broker, imposing a registry or resuming the deferred
threads design. Implementation and operating-policy decisions remain with
their owners.

**Owner and recheck:** market-research-expert. Recheck on controlled
routing-agent studies or outcome data for Projects-style coordinators.
Update or supersede this dated conclusion and log the change when the
comparison becomes answerable; missing evidence here is not proof that no
relevant study exists anywhere.
