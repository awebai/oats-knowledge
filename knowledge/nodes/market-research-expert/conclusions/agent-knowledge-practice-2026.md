# Dated conclusion

Compare **producing a knowledge revision**, **adopting it into shared use**
and **measuring its effect** separately. A background consolidation job can
produce a candidate without making it authoritative; a PR or review screen
can expose a choice without proving the quality or cost of that choice.
The source's **2026-11-02** investigation reports examples of these separate
stages, not a measured best review design or a universal vendor contract.

This distinction matters when comparing knowledge-promotion workflows:
record what is revised, who can adopt the result, and whether the evaluation
measures useful retained knowledge, reviewer effort or merely completed jobs.
Scheduled maintenance alone establishes none of those outcomes.

# Review-design questions remain open

This captured investigation supplies neither a head-to-head comparison of
**a separate knowledge judge followed by a reviewer** against **direct
proposal to that reviewer**, nor measurements of review effort or approval
rates specifically for agent-proposed knowledge. Those are investigation
limits, not proof that no relevant study exists. The short note retains no
reproducible search inventory supporting a universal negative claim.

Future comparisons should measure review effort and retained-knowledge
quality rather than infer effectiveness from the existence of a judge,
maintenance schedule or PR. General agent-code review findings in the
[human-oversight conclusion](/nodes/market-research-expert/conclusions/human-oversight-evidence-2026.md)
do not supply a knowledge-review outcome comparison. The
[verification lesson](/nodes/market-research-expert/lessons/verification-needs-independent-evidence.md)
owns reviewer-evidence independence; the
[scaffolding lesson](/nodes/market-research-expert/lessons/retest-scaffolding-after-model-changes.md)
owns model-dependent retesting and persistence versus learning.

Nothing here selects or removes an OATS judge or reviewer, changes promotion
doctrine, or supersedes the accepted
[durable-specialization rationale](/nodes/oats-maintainer/decisions/durable-specialization.md).
The [decisive-claim verification lesson](/nodes/market-research-expert/lessons/verify-decisive-product-claims-before-synthesis.md)
applies before turning the reported examples into present-tense product claims.
