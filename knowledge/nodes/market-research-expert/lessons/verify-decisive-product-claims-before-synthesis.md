# Lesson and evidence boundary

Budget a separate primary-source verification pass for the claims a market
conclusion depends on: retirements, replacement products, renames, approval
or merge gates, and headline numbers. Broad research can locate a useful
lead without establishing the precise claim that survives into a synthesis.
A confident summary or repeated search snippet is not an independent check.

The source reports that its **2026-11-02** pass corrected or narrowed several
such claims. Its captured examples distinguish:

- a shutdown from a migration into a named successor;
- an actual migration target from one supplied by secondary reporting;
- a UI request to merge from an enforced merge gate;
- maintenance with a successor from abandonment;
- self-reported intent from observed behavior.

These are source-reported correction categories, not verified product
histories. The captured note does not include the primary pages or an
itemized before/after record, so this finding retains neither an exact
correction count nor a correction rate.

# Repeated research can preserve a source while losing its meaning

A separate verification proposal, also dated 2026-11-02, reports rechecking
claims on which same-model research instances had converged. It found real
sources behind the claims but identified these distortion categories:

- mixing a paper's version-specific results or category definitions;
- combining results from separate posts into a single apparent range;
- presenting a vendor product-post experiment as an academic study;
- generalizing a worst-case variant or hard-setting result to all tasks;
- adding qualifiers such as exclusivity, model-family scope or universality
  that the source did not establish;
- sampling products without looking outside the initial set for
  counterexamples.

This is an attributed process finding, not an independently audited
before/after inventory or a general error rate. The captured note supplies
examples, but the full verification report and primary pages are not part
of the evidence inspected by this harvest. The source also warns that its
page-fetch tool returned model-generated summaries, sometimes only quote
fragments; a fragment labelled verbatim is not thereby a checked quotation.
Repeated agreement among research instances is not independent confirmation.

A second pass, also dated 2026-11-02, adds two specific failure modes:
**moving results between experiments inside one paper**, and **replacing an
operational measure with a broader interpretation**. The source reports a
ProgramBench model comparison being attributed to a separate Claude Code
session sample, and absence of review comments being relabelled as absence
of review. It also reports conclusions contradicting the supporting passage
already present in the research summary. These are source-reported examples,
not an audited correction rate; the individual review statistics are not
established by this note. Earlier worst-case and self-forecast distortions
are recurrence, not independent evidence for their numerical claims.

A third pass, also on 2026-11-02, reports additional distortions **between
research and the synthesized brief**: fusing two papers into one causal
story, broadening a forge/catalog-scoped finding into a claim about all
tools, and presenting one vendor's design as cross-vendor convergence. It
also reports misdescribing an LLM-based security pipeline's findings as all
formally checked, and failing to locate a claimed figure in its cited paper.
These are attributed verification findings, not an independently audited
absence proof or evidence that all the underlying sources were fabricated.
The [topology lesson](/nodes/market-research-expert/lessons/topology-gains-depend-on-task-and-budget.md)
owns the separated seam/interference study results; the
[protocol-field lesson](/nodes/market-research-expert/lessons/human-request-fields-need-explicit-verification.md)
owns the A2A method and identity qualifications, rather than duplicating
those accounts here.

# Refinement: the comparator can reverse a saving headline

A source-reported verification pass on **2026-11-03** revisited a two-effort
OATS coordination fixture run on 2026-11-02/03. Its recommended gate arm
looked cheaper in reported human minutes than the single no-gate run but
more expensive than the existing-process control. The source reports that
the additional human authorization work was deliberate. The accepted
[gate-evaluation lesson](/nodes/oats-expert/lessons/evaluate-gates-beyond-code-seed-catches.md)
already owns the trial observations and scripted-human accounting limits;
this refinement adds the consequence for research claims, not another trial
result or a new gate recommendation.

Before retaining a time-saving headline, name **which comparator** supports
it and whether it is the current process or a deliberately altered arm.
Preserve the denominator and distinguish human-time accounting, elapsed
runtime, request counts and actual decisions. State which approval duties
move to or from the human: fewer minutes under different authority
assignments are not an isolated efficiency or safety effect. When available
comparators reverse the headline, show that contrast rather than silently
choosing the favorable baseline. More human decisions may be an intentional
authorization cost, not automatically a design failure.

The new capture contains one human seat, two efforts, three runs per main
arm, one no-gate run and unequal planted seeds. It does not retain a full
timing ledger or measured model identifier. The proposer reports reading
the trial results and a revised page; this harvest read only the captured
proposal and named note. Neither that rereading nor the reported reversal
establishes observed real-user savings, sustainable supervisory capacity,
safety superiority or a product outcome. Do not turn the no-gate comparison
into an unqualified claim that the gate halves human time.

# Refinement: upstream corrections can fail to reach the final page

A separate **2026-11-03** proposal reports auditing one synthesized decision
page after adversarial verification of its research. The source found
claims still using categories or interpretations that the verification
record had already corrected, and real figures attached to the wrong paper.
In particular, it reports a WideSWE feature result attributed to CooperBench
despite the earlier record assigning it to WideSWE. A valid number found
somewhere in the research is not support from the paper the final text cites.
The [WideSWE account](/nodes/market-research-expert/limitations/cross-repo-architecture-ownership-evidence-gap.md)
owns that study's results and limits; they are not a new benchmark finding here.

The source reports reading the primary texts, but this harvest inspected
only its proposal and named note, not the page, full audit, earlier verdicts
or papers. This is one page and one audit, with the audit model identified
by the source as `github-copilot/claude-opus-5.5`; no independent replication,
general error rate or measured benefit from a final audit is established.
The added process finding is **correction propagation failure**: checking
the research does not establish that a later synthesis preserved its verdicts.

# Consequence for research

For decisive claims, check what the accessible primary source actually says
and preserve its scope. A retirement announcement does not automatically
name a successor or establish why the product was withdrawn. A button or
human-sent instruction does not, by itself, establish an enforced authority
boundary. A reported intention is not a measured action.

For each decisive figure, preserve the source version, measured population
and denominator, task/model/experimental condition, and the supporting
wording. Check those fields against the accessible primary text, not just a
helper's summary. Mark wording obtained through a summarizer as unverified
until compared with the original. Check whether a strong qualifier actually
appears, keep separate studies and versions separate, and deliberately search
for counterexamples outside the consensus sample. Record the **experiment
or table identifier and the measured variable's definition** as well as the
paper identifier: a correct paper citation can still attach the wrong
sample, model or outcome. Absence of a recorded action is not automatically
absence of the underlying activity. Check the conclusion against its own
supporting passage before treating consensus as corroboration. Recheck scope
at each synthesis handoff: a brief must not broaden a bounded finding merely
by dropping the product category or attributing one vendor's choice to the
market. A causal story needs a matching intervention and population, not two
compatible-looking headlines. Record whether a security result was proposed
by a model, checked in a formal model or tested in an implementation. If a
figure cannot be located in the cited version, exclude it pending a traceable
source rather than fill in a plausible origin.

Before treating a decision-bearing synthesis as reviewed, audit the **final
artifact itself** against both the primary text and the earlier verification
verdicts. For each decisive figure, confirm that the cited paper and
experiment contain it, rather than accepting a match in a neighboring paper.
Trace each relevant correction into the final wording and source list,
including narrowed categories, reattributions and exclusions. An upstream
corrected verdict is not evidence that the published sentence was corrected.
Resolve discrepancies against the primary source; do not treat the earlier
verdict as infallible or substitute citation presence for semantic agreement.

If a first-party page cannot be inspected, including because a fetch returns
403, label any claim supported only by press or another secondary source as
such; do not upgrade its evidence status because the secondary source links
to the blocked page. If no usable support remains, leave the claim unknown.
When an undated changelog is assigned a date from a package registry's
publication time, mark that date as an inference, not a date stated by the
changelog. Do not generalize one access failure into a permanent property
of a vendor's domain.

This lesson concerns **accuracy and evidentiary precision**, distinct from
[fetched content's lack of instructional authority](/nodes/market-research-expert/lessons/fetched-research-is-untrusted-data.md).
The [supervision lesson](/nodes/market-research-expert/lessons/supervision-patterns-are-not-outcome-evidence.md)
separately owns the distinction between shipped interfaces and measured
human outcomes, and the
[question-routing lesson](/nodes/market-research-expert/lessons/question-routing-is-not-approval-authority.md)
owns how to compare who carries a request with who may approve the action. Neither a verification pass nor a first-party statement
substitutes for a runtime test where behavior or enforcement is the claim.

# Elimination route and ownership

**Owner:** market-research-expert. The reusable verification step belongs in
this soul's research and delegation guidance, for separate review; no soul
skill is changed here. Structured evidence records can bind claims to paper
and experiment identifiers, verification verdicts and final-artifact locations;
checks can flag missing citations, evidence labels or unresolved corrections.
These are workflow improvements to review, not an implemented or proven defense.
They cannot determine whether the final wording faithfully preserves the
source's meaning, including shutdown, migration or authority interpretations.
That remaining semantic check requires source review, not a lint rule alone.

Revisit this lesson when the research workflow records and checks decisive
claims systematically. Update or supersede it and log the change without
turning the reported incident into an independently audited result.
