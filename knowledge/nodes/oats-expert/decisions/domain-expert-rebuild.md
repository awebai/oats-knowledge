# Rationale

The accepted rebuild organizes expertise around overall OATS direction, kernel contracts, Desktop judgment, sourced market research and practical adoption. Its five persistent souls are oats-expert, oats-kernel-expert, oats-desktop-expert, market-research-expert and oats-assistant. Knowledge-theory-expert remains an optional authoring package, not a sixth required persistent role.

An expert may implement when assigned. Implementation is a task, not a reason to preserve engineer identities or create one expert per source module. A separate documentation or coordination role is not needed merely to retain useful writing or review procedures.

Each concept has one external canonical home; other domains cross-read it. In particular, Desktop owns its product succession rationale and overall planning reads that decision. Keep only useful current rationale, rejected alternatives and discovered limits; code descriptions and raw delivery ledgers do not become expertise by relocation.

The rebuild follows implementation and deployment acceptance. Preserve pending evidence and coordinate old writers before switching bindings, then verify fresh readers before separately decommissioning sources. No bulk migration or active legacy attic substitutes for selection.
