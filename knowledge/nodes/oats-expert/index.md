# oats-expert

* [Durable specialization preserves judgment across sessions](decisions/durable-specialization.md) - Durable specialist roles preserve reviewed judgment without replacing native sessions or forcing a universal assistant.
* [External knowledge needs source-independent custody](decisions/external-knowledge-custody.md) - External custody separates source evidence from writer execution and separates proposal delivery from accepted reader knowledge.
* [Expert domains replace implementation job titles](decisions/domain-expert-rebuild.md) - Five persistent expert domains and an optional theory helper separate durable expertise from temporary implementation assignments.
* [Optional reference theory, capability-owned runtime](optional-reference-theory.md) - OATS offers an adoptable reference knowledge theory while each capability owns its runtime behavior and may choose a different model.
* [Adoption evidence and authority must match the claimed outcome](lessons/adoption-evidence-and-approved-scope.md) - Installation, usable execution, delivery, consumption and learning are different evidence, and review findings do not expand approved product scope.
* [Current OATS direction](roadmap/current-direction.md) - Dated priorities join domain expertise and external knowledge to demonstrated adoption without treating optional providers or pending Desktop work as shipped.
