# Ownership and freshness

**Owner: oats-expert. Last verified: 2026-09-13.** This is a planning snapshot,
not a delivery ledger. When accepted human priorities or verified delivery
reality change, the owner must propose an update through knowledge PR review
in the same session, revise the date and append the reason to the node/base
logs. Recheck the baseline before using this snapshot to claim current support.

# Published baseline

OATS **0.23.1** is published at `b5e233b98bd6e1fa13e62319910cad5caf6defaa`;
both kernel and Pi npm packages identify that commit. Standalone OKF **2.0.0**

not establish that a deployment has upgraded, activated it or completed its
knowledge cutover.

# Accepted priorities and open work

- **Domain experts and external expertise:** carry out the
  [domain-expert rebuild](/nodes/oats-expert/decisions/domain-expert-rebuild.md)
  with one canonical concept home, specialist cross-reads and
  [source-independent custody](/nodes/oats-expert/decisions/external-knowledge-custody.md).
  Preserve pending evidence before rebinding; verify accepted knowledge in
  fresh role instances before separately decommissioning old sources.
- **Adoption proof:** demonstrate practical onboarding and useful expert
  answers at the user's boundary. A public helper completing a task is not
  proof that a persistent assistant learned. Keep execution, reviewed delivery,
  fresh-reader consumption and learning as
  [separate evidence](/nodes/oats-expert/lessons/adoption-evidence-and-approved-scope.md).
- **Optional alternatives:** alternate knowledge providers, including
  Omnigraph as a motivating example, remain options to investigate—not a
  committed implementation or a verified integration. Preserve the accepted
  [optional reference theory](/nodes/oats-expert/optional-reference-theory.md)
  rather than imposing its model on every capability.
- **Sourced market research needed:** market-research-expert owns dated,
  attributable investigation before comparisons or positioning claims are
  promoted. No competitor capability, ranking or market finding is established
  by this roadmap; unknown is not absent.
- **Desktop UX remains pending:** follow-up work is delegated through a private
  handoff, not reported shipped by this knowledge change or inferred from the
  release baseline. Keep task details in that handoff. Desktop owns product
  judgment under the existing
  [product/CLI-authority decision](/nodes/oats-desktop-expert/decisions/standalone-product-and-cli-authority.md);
  oats-expert updates this status only against reviewed delivery evidence.
