# Decision and acceptance

On 2026-11-05 the directing human told the release lane that OATS is not
to be announced publicly yet: the project needs a lot more polish before
it is posted to a public forum. He told the other side's human the same
day, when that side was preparing a Hacker News launch.

Acceptance is **source-reported**: Pepe, directly to the release lane in
session on 2026-11-05. No public record of his statement was cited; this
harvest read none. What counts as polished enough was still open at
capture and waits on his list. This decision records the direction and its
review consequence, not a launch date, a polish checklist or a roadmap
item.

# What it means for the release lane

A public launch is a direction call that belongs to the project's humans
together, not to one lane's effort or to one side. Until the humans agree a
launch:

- Work framed as "for the launch" (onboarding wording, README changes,
  launch-day urgency) is judged on its own merits, without launch urgency.
  Urgency attributed to a launch is not a reason to shorten review, skip a
  second read or merge ahead of the usual gates.
- A request that presents a launch as already decided goes back to the
  humans for the joint call rather than being treated as direction the lane
  can act on.

The lane's review and release gates are unchanged by this decision; it only
removes a particular reason that had been offered for hurrying them. The
[release-lane arrangement](/nodes/oats-maintainer/stewardship/release-lane-and-project-lanes.md)
still owns who reviews and merges; the
[release-only review decision](/nodes/oats-maintainer/stewardship/onboarding-redesign-release-only-review.md)
still owns the onboarding redesign's review.

# What exposed it

The source reports that a README onboarding change,
[oats-project/oats#1745](https://github.com/oats-project/oats/pull/1745),
was asked for urgently as being for a Hacker News launch that day and was
merged. The directing human then said the project was not ready to be
announced. The PR was read as merged during harvest, and its description
carries the launch framing; the human's statement and its timing relative
to the merge are source-reported. The merged change itself is not judged
here, and nothing in this decision asks for it to be reverted.
